The reality

Most nights are fine. Until one isn't.

You get home. You laugh about it. You do it all again next week. But this isn't about the nights that go right.

It's about the one that doesn't. The moment someone goes too quiet. Someone drops. Or panic kicks in and nobody knows what to do next.

That moment matters. What you do in it matters too.

{{ photo "friends-checking-on-mate" "hero" "rounded" }}

Why we built this

We built the thing we thought was missing

We've all said it. Look after your mates. But too often that's where it ends.

No one shows you how to step in when someone won't wake up. When someone's overheating. When someone starts fitting. When someone's panicking out of their mind. So we built this for the moments that matter. Not to preach. Not to tell you how to live. Just so nobody's left stood there guessing when a mate needs help.

Built by ravers. For ravers. For mates. For families. For anyone who gives a shit.

Who this is for

For the people who care

The ones who check in. The ones who hang back. The ones who would rather know than guess. If you've ever thought "I wouldn't know what to do if this went sideways", this is for you. And for your mum, your carer, or anyone with a mate who wants the confidence to say "I've got you".

The groups

For the mates making memories together. The laughers, the dancers, the ones who came out for a good night.

The lookouts

For the one who always ends up keeping an eye on everyone else. The mate who notices when something's off.

The ones waiting at home

For parents, partners, brothers, sisters and mates. Anyone who wants their people home safe.

The takeaway

You don't need to know everything

You just need to know enough to step in. Stay calm. Be honest. Get help early and don't leave your mate on their own. That alone can change everything.

Someone not waking up, overheating or fitting? Call 999. Then see What To Do
