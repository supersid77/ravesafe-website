1. Who we are

This website is run by RaveSafe Community Ltd, a company limited by guarantee registered in England and Wales (company number 16396150). Our registered office is 11 Eastbourne Road, Walton, Liverpool, L9 0JE.

We're the data controller for the personal information covered by this policy. That means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for looking after it. This policy covers ravesafecommunity.com and ravesafe.app.

For anything about your data, email miss.k@ravesafecommunity.com with "Privacy" in the subject line.

2. The short version

  • You can read the whole site without giving us any personal details.
  • We don't use advertising, tracking pixels or analytics cookies.
  • We only collect what you choose to send us through our forms or by email.
  • We never sell your information.
  • You can ask to see, change or delete what we hold at any time.

4. Sensitive information

Some information, like details about health or drug use, is treated as extra sensitive under the law. Please don't send us that kind of information about yourself or anyone else through our forms or Miss K chat unless you're happy for us to have it.

If you choose to include it in a community story, we'll only use it with your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the permission box. If you'd rather it wasn't shared, keep your story anonymous and leave out anything that could identify you or anyone else. We'll remove identifying details before publishing anything.

Miss K is an automated chat and isn't monitored in real time. If someone is in danger, call 999.

5. Who we share it with

We only share your information with companies that help us run the website, and only what they need to do their job. They're not allowed to use it for anything else.

  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Web3Forms takes what you send through our contact, volunteer and story forms and emails it to us.
  • Hostinger provides our email, so it holds emails you send us, our replies, and the form messages Web3Forms sends to our inbox.
  • Chatbase runs Miss K chat. Your messages are processed by Chatbase and the AI provider it uses to write replies.
  • YouTube plays the recovery position video on our What To Do page. Nothing is sent to YouTube unless you press play. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

We'll also share information if the law requires it, or if we need to protect someone's life in an emergency. We never sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Links to other websites, like drug testing services and support charities, take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

6. Data stored outside the UK

Some of our providers are based in, or store data in, the USA or the EU. Where that happens, we make sure there are proper safeguards in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU's standard contractual clauses, or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Get a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Have anything wrong corrected
  • Have your information deleted
  • Ask us to limit how we use it
  • Object to us using it
  • Get your information in a format you can move elsewhere
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, for example to take down a story

To use any of these rights, email miss.k@ravesafecommunity.com. It's free, and we'll reply within one month. We may need to check who you are first.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Age

Our forms are for people aged 16 and over. If you're under 16, please ask a parent, carer or another adult you trust to get in touch for you. If we find we've collected information from someone under 16 without that, we'll delete it.

10. Keeping it safe

The website runs over a secure, encrypted connection. Only the people who need to see your information can, and we keep our accounts locked down. No website is completely secure, but if anything ever goes wrong we'll act quickly and tell you and the ICO when the law requires it.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if anything changes, like adding a new service, and change the date at the top. If it's a big change that affects information you've already given us, we'll tell you.
