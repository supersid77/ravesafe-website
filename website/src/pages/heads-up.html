If your mate isn't right

Overheating, struggling to breathe, chest pain, fitting, collapsing, drifting in and out, or can't be woken properly? Get help straight away.

What to do

  1. Stay with them

    Tell staff, or call 999 if it's serious.

  2. Be honest

    Tell medics what they may have taken. They need facts, not guesswork.

  3. Protect their airway

    If they're unconscious but breathing, put them in the recovery position.

Why we do Heads Up

We're not here to shout alerts for the sake of it. We're here to show people the truth about what's doing the rounds. The aim is simple: encourage people to test, plan in advance, and make testing part of the ritual. With habit, it becomes a small move with a massive benefit.
