The small print

Cookie policy

Short version. We don't track you. That's why there's no cookie banner on this site.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves on your phone or computer. Similar tools, like local storage, do the same job. Some are needed for a site to work. Others track what you do so you can be shown adverts or so a site can count its visitors.

What we use

Our website doesn't set any cookies. We don't use analytics, advertising or social media tracking. Our fonts are stored on our own website, so loading a page doesn't send your details to anyone else.

A few things you can choose to use come from other services. They only load when you tap them, and they may store something on your device when you do.

ServiceWhen it's usedWhat it stores and why
YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode)Only when you press play on the recovery position video on What To DoYouTube may store information to play the video and remember your settings. See Google's cookie policy.
Miss K chat (Chatbase)Only when you open the chatStores a reference so your conversation carries on as you move between pages. See the privacy policy on Chatbase's website.

Because these are only used when you ask for them, they count as strictly necessary and don't need a separate consent pop-up. If you'd rather not use them, just don't press play or open the chat. You can still watch the video on YouTube itself, or contact us instead of using the chat.

Things our pages remember

Some tools on our pages, like the festival checklist and the RaveSafe ID maker, work entirely on your device. What you type into them isn't sent to us and isn't saved once you leave the page.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them won't stop our website working, though the video and chat may not load.

Changes

If we ever add cookies that need your consent, like visitor counting, we'll update this page and ask you first.

Questions? Email miss.k@ravesafecommunity.com. See also our privacy policy.
