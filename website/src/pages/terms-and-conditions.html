2. What our information is, and isn't

RaveSafe is a harm reduction project. We give honest, practical information to help people stay safer and look after each other.

  • It's general information for education. It isn't medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace a doctor, paramedic, pharmacist or welfare team.
  • It doesn't encourage anyone to take drugs or break the law. The safest choice is not to take drugs at all. Our information is for people who need to know how to reduce the risks.
  • Drugs vary in strength and content. Nothing on this site can tell you what's in a particular substance or how it will affect you.
  • Drug alerts and testing information come from other organisations. We share them in good faith but can't guarantee they're complete or up to date.
  • Always be honest with medics about what's been taken. They're there to help, not to judge.

We work hard to keep everything accurate, but information changes. If you spot something wrong, please tell us.

3. Who the site is for

Our content is written for people aged 16 and over, and for parents, carers and professionals. Our forms are for people aged 16 and over.

4. Miss K chat

Miss K is an automated chat assistant trained on harm reduction information. She isn't a person, a medic or a counsellor, and she can get things wrong. Don't use her in an emergency, and don't share personal details like your full name, address or medical history. Check anything important with a professional or one of the support services we list.

  • Hack, overload or copy the site with automated tools

We can remove anything that breaks these rules and block anyone who keeps breaking them.
