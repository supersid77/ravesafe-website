How it works

Spot the signs. Learn through story.

1. Pick a planet

Each planet is built around the substance it represents. The world itself shows you how it feels and where it goes wrong.

2. Meet the characters

Follow their night from the first rush to the moment it tips. Every character was human on Planet Plush before the planet changed them.

3. Learn the way back

Every chapter ends with real steps: what to look out for, how to help a mate and when to get medics.

For parents, teachers and youth workers

A way into the conversation

The Ketaverse is harm reduction education. It doesn't encourage drug use. It gives young people a way to understand the risks that doesn't feel like a telling off.

Every planet points back to real-world help: what to look out for, how to look after a mate and when to get medical help. If you want to talk it through with someone you care about, Rave Parents has guidance on starting that conversation.
