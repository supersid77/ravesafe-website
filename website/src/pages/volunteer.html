Volunteer with us

Join the RaveSafe community

Help build safer dancefloors online, on site and after the event. We're a community harm reduction project built on two simple ideas. Leave no one behind. Leave no trace.

Calm, kind and practical. That's all we ask.

The basics

What volunteering looks like

What you'll do

  • Support event crews
  • Help people find welfare, medics, water and safe spaces
  • Share approved safety content
  • Help out with training
  • Rescue and reuse camping gear after festivals

Scope and safety

You won't give medical care unless you're qualified to. The role is early support, calm communication and getting people to welfare, medics or emergency services.

Time you give

It's flexible. Give an hour online, help at one event, or join a logistics day. Whatever fits around your life.
