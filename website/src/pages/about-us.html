About us

Why we exist

We're here to close the safety gaps

RaveSafe joins up the before, during and after of a night out. We don't replace medics or welfare teams. We help people spot problems sooner and get them to the right help faster.

The problem

Things have changed and the risks are real

  • Substances are stronger and less predictable than they used to be
  • Spiking is a growing risk
  • Early warning signs get missed
  • Stigma stops people asking for help
  • Friends often don't know what to do

Quick, informed action saves lives. We give people calm, clear steps to look after their mates. No judgement. No lectures.

What we're building

Safety for the whole night

One place that covers every stage, from packing your bag to getting home and getting over it.

Before

Prep and awareness

  • Festival checklists and kit lists
  • Harm reduction advice
  • Drug alerts
  • Planning tips

During

In-the-moment safety

  • Step-by-step "what to do if" guides
  • Prompts for when to call 999
  • Where to find welfare and medics
  • A look-after-your-mates mindset

After

Recovery and support

  • Aftercare and comedown guidance
  • Welfare and support services
  • Free guides and workbooks
  • Space to reflect

What makes us different

Built from experience, not theory

  • Made to be used in real time, not read at home
  • Backed by festival, nightlife and frontline response experience
  • Community first, not profit first
  • Simple enough to follow under pressure

Why it works

When someone's unwell, nobody wants a wall of text. They need a plan. Everything we make answers three questions: what's happening, what could it mean, and what do I do next?
